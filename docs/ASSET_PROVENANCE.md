## Active-use decision

The first cinematic direction was rejected during visual review because the
abstract 3D gate and generated films did not explain the product. Those two
MP4 files and the old evidence-core GLB remain inactive provenance and are not
referenced by the current interface or metadata.

The replacement uses two static, composition-locked frames in ProofRail's own
method section. Playback is initiated by the user and bound to the real gate:
a blocked workspace stops at the human gate and cannot reveal the release-ready
frame. The sequence does not mutate claims, evidence, approval, the gate, or a
receipt. The Meshy dossier is also poster-first and explicitly labelled as a
geometry study. The page therefore still loads **no video, WebGL canvas,
Three.js runtime, or interactive 3D model**. A GLB export was requested, but its
browser transfer was blocked by the local Chrome extension's file-access
permission and is not claimed as integrated.

The replacement visual system is authored from ProofRail's real states:
publication preview, claim underline, evidence relationship, human decision,
and publish gate. MotionSites member examples including Blog Showcase,
LaunchEx, NexaCore, Email Landing Page, and Urban Jungle were consulted through
the user's account for editorial scale, asymmetric composition, workflow logic,
and controlled transitions. No MotionSites prompt text, source code, hosted
media, or hotlinked asset is copied into this repository.
