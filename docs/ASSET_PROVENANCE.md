## Retired media directions

Earlier cinematic and 3D explorations are retired from the product direction.
Higgsfield and Meshy are not used in the active experience because neither
medium made the live claim → source → proposed wording → human decision →
release-status relationship clearer than the interface itself.

No video, generated workflow frame, 3D model, 3D poster, or previous social card
is an active product dependency. No additional generation is planned merely
because account credits are available.

MotionSites was reviewed only through its public, read-only reference surface.
No protected prompt, premium template, code, brand system, image, or video was
copied into ProofRail; the application layout and assets in this repository are
original to this rebuild.

## Runtime boundary

- Publication content and evidence never inherit this social card as source
  material.
- The active application must not load generated video or a 3D runtime.
- Generated media cannot establish approval, gate success, or receipt validity.
- Any future generated asset requires an explicit purpose, live-state mapping,
  fallback, provenance record, performance budget, accessibility treatment, and
  human review before integration.
